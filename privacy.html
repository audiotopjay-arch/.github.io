Privacy Policy — Audiotop Hermes

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Audiotop Hermes is a private, single-user assistant operated by Audiotop (Taipei, Taiwan) for its owner's own use. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit consent, the app reads the owner's Gmail messages using the read-only scope (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, modify, or delete email.

Use

Email content is used only to summarize, organize, and draft replies for the owner. Drafts are shown to the owner and are never sent by the app.

Storage and sharing

Data is processed on the owner's own computer and sent to the AI model provider (Anthropic) solely to generate responses. It is not sold, shared with other parties, or used for advertising. Google user data is handled in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
